Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick solutions to common questions regarding portals, enrollments, exams, and attendance.

How do I register for courses?

Visit the Courses page and click on the course you want to enroll in. Choose your elective options and click "Confirm Registration" before the semester deadline.

How can I check my attendance?

Go to the Attendance page from your dashboard to view course-wise attendance percentage and overall lectures attended.

When are results published?

Results are typically published within 2 weeks after the examination period ends. You can check subject-wise grade sheets on the Results page.

How do I reset my password?

Contact the admin office at support@studenthub.edu.in or use the password recovery link on the Login page.

Can I change my enrolled courses?

Yes, course changes and elective modifications are allowed within the first 2 weeks of the semester through your academic counselor.

How do I contact my professors?

Visit the Courses page to find faculty email addresses and department office cabin numbers.

What is the minimum attendance requirement?

A minimum of 75% attendance is strictly required in each subject to be eligible for end-semester examinations.

How do I submit assignments online?

Navigate to the Assignments page, locate the pending assignment task, and click "Upload" to attach your files.

Still have questions?

Can't find the answer you're looking for? Reach out to our campus student support center and our team will assist you.
