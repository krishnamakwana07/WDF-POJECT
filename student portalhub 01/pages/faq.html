Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register for courses?

Visit the Courses page and click on the course you want to enroll in.

How can I check my attendance?

Go to the Attendance page from your dashboard to view course-wise attendance.

When are results published?

Results are typically published within 2 weeks after the examination period ends.

How do I reset my password?

Contact the admin office or use the forgot password link on the login page.

Can I change my enrolled courses?

Yes, course changes are allowed within the first 2 weeks of the semester.

How do I contact my professors?

Visit the Courses page to find faculty contact details.

What is the minimum attendance requirement?

A minimum of 75% attendance is required in each course.

How do I submit assignments online?

Go to the Assignments page and click Submit next to the assignment.

Still have questions?

Feel free to contact us and we will get back to you as soon as possible.
